About the game

Welcome to the sandbox

A physics sandbox where experimentation is the point.

Nothing is set in stone.

Infinite Sandbox lets you spawn characters and objects, experiment with physics, and create your own situations.

Try different weapons, test explosions, explore levels, and discover what happens when everything interacts.

There is no single way to play. Set up an experiment, build something, or just see what happens.
